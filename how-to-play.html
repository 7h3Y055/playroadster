How to Play Roadster Dash

Official Gameplay Manual · Mechanics, Controls & High-Score Strategies

Welcome to the official driver manual for Roadster Dash. Whether you are playing on a desktop with a mechanical keyboard or on your phone with touch swipe gestures, this guide will teach you the fundamentals of supersonic lane shifting, thruster energy management, combo multipliers, and survival techniques.

1. The Objective

Your goal is simple yet demanding: survive as long as possible down the vibrant 4-lane neon causeway, dodge oncoming obstacles, leap over bumper-to-bumper traffic using cold-gas thrusters, collect high-value gold career tokens, and achieve the highest distance and score possible.

3. Authentic Cold-Gas Thruster Dynamics

The centerpiece of the Tesla Roadster's handling is its SpaceX Cold-Gas Thruster System. Mounted behind the license plate and beneath the chassis, these composite overwrapped pressure vessels (COPV) release compressed nitrogen gas for instantaneous vertical lift.

  • Air Tank Gauge: Your HUD displays an AIR meter that drains while thrusters fire.
  • Automatic Recharge: Once you touch back down on the road, your onboard compressor rapidly refills the cold-gas pressure tank to 100%.
  • Precision Dive Slam: If you are floating in the air and see an upcoming bridge or airborne obstacle, press ↓ or swipe down to slam back to ground level immediately.

4. Scoring, Combos & Multipliers

Your total score is a combination of distance covered, speed scaling, near-miss bonuses, and token collection:

  • Distance Points: Points tick up continuously as you hurtle forward. The faster your car moves, the more points awarded per meter.
  • Near-Miss Combos: Passing within inches of a sports sedan or truck without colliding triggers a NEAR MISS combo multiplier (×2, ×3, up to ×5!).
  • Fever Mode: Chaining combos fills your ROCKET FEVER gauge. When full, you achieve temporary invincibility and supersonic speed.

5. Obstacle Catalog

The Miami causeway is populated with diverse traffic patterns:

  • Sports Sedans: Fast-moving cruisers that switch lanes dynamically.
  • Delivery Vans & Taxis: Slower vehicles that form walls across multiple lanes. Jump over them!
  • Heavy Semi-Trucks: Long vehicles that require a full thruster hover to clear from bumper to cab.
  • Highway Ramps: Yellow launch wedges that catapult you high into the air, enabling massive stunt combos and bonus tokens.

⚡ Pro Driver Secret

Always preserve at least 25% of your AIR gauge for emergency evasions. If two lanes are blocked by semi-trucks, a quick thruster jump is the only way to avoid a fatal crash!
