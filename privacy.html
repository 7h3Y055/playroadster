ROADSTER DASH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · play-roadster.live

Welcome to Roadster Dash ("play-roadster.live", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit and play our web game.

1. Information We Collect

Roadster Dash is a browser-based 3D endless runner. We do not require account registration, usernames, passwords, or personal email addresses to play.

  • Local Storage: We use your browser's LocalStorage exclusively to store your personal gameplay records (high score, career tokens, sound settings, and graphics quality preference). This data stays entirely on your device and is never sent to our servers.
  • Technical & Device Data: Like most web applications, our hosting infrastructure may automatically receive basic technical information such as your browser type, device operating system, language preference, and IP address for site stability and security.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

We use third-party advertising partners, notably Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Google and its partner advertising networks use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Analytics

We may use standard web analytics to understand game performance, load times, and aggregated player count to help us optimize framerates and gameplay mechanics across different devices.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Roadster Dash is a general audience casual game and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback about this Privacy Policy or Roadster Dash, please reach out via GitHub at github.com/7h3Y055/playroadster.
