About Roadster Dash

Next-Generation 3D Browser Highway Runner · WebGL & Three.js

Roadster Dash is an adrenaline-fueled 3D endless runner playable directly in your web browser with zero installs or downloads. Set along a neon-soaked, retro-futuristic Miami causeway at dusk, players take the helm of a futuristic electric sports car outfitted with authentic SpaceX cold-gas thrusters, maneuvering through high-speed traffic, performing aerial hover stunts, and setting high score records.

The Inspiration

Roadster Dash was born out of a passion for high-speed arcade racing, 1980s synthwave aesthetics, and the frontier of aerospace engineering. Inspired by Elon Musk's legendary announcement of the Tesla Roadster SpaceX Package—featuring high-pressure air thrusters capable of dramatic acceleration and short hops—Roadster Dash turns that concept into an exhilarating, fast-paced arcade reality.

Unlike conventional lane-runners that limit players to purely 2D horizontal movement, Roadster Dash incorporates true verticality. Using an authentic 10-nozzle cold-gas thruster flight simulation, you can jump over multi-car pileups, hover over cruising semi-trucks, and execute precision dive-slams onto highway ramps.

Core Game Engine & Architecture

Custom 3D WebGL Engine

Built using modern Three.js and Vite, rendering dynamic lighting, neon road reflections, and procedural synthwave palm trees at a locked 60 FPS.

Cold-Gas Physics Simulation

Authentic thruster thrust, gravity falloff, and mid-air hover dynamics driven by fixed-step physics integration for responsive, tactile controls.

Universal Device Optimization

Engineered for seamless play across iOS Safari, Android Chrome, Windows, and Mac with fluid swipe gestures, keyboard mapping, and 4 graphics modes.

Zero-Install Web App

Full Progressive Web App (PWA) support allowing you to install Roadster Dash directly to your home screen and play in full-screen immersion.

Creator & Mission

Roadster Dash is created and actively maintained by Youssef Bouchmama. The mission behind the project is to prove that modern web technologies (WebGL, Web Audio API, TypeScript) can deliver console-quality arcade experiences instantly in the browser without friction, paywalls, or multi-gigabyte app downloads.

We believe in open web standards, transparent privacy, and fast, accessible gaming for players around the world.
