Reporting a Bug?

To help us diagnose and fix issues as quickly as possible, please include:

  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, Samsung Galaxy S23, M2 MacBook Pro)
  • Your browser version (e.g., iOS Safari 17.4, Chrome 124)
  • Your active graphics quality setting (Low, Mid, High, Ultra)
  • A brief description of what happened and steps to reproduce
